🍟 Diet Don'ts — Cut These Out
🧂
Extra salt & high-sodium food (chips, packaged snacks, instant noodles)
Causes massive water retention in face → puffiness and soft jaw
🍬
Sugar — cold drinks, biscuits, sweets, fruit juice, desserts
Insulin spike → inflammation → puffiness + oilier skin + more blackheads
🍺
Alcohol — completely eliminate
Dehydrates skin, causes facial bloating, destroys sleep quality
🥛
Excess dairy (especially milk) — try cutting for 3 weeks
Hormones in milk can worsen acne and blackheads for many people
🍞
Refined carbs — white bread, maida products, too much white rice
Spikes insulin → triggers oilier skin
🚫 Skincare Don'ts — Stop Immediately
🤚
Touching your face constantly
Transfers bacteria and oils from hands → clogs pores → blackheads every time
😖
Squeezing blackheads / pimples
Pushes bacteria deeper, causes permanent scarring and enlarged pores
🩹
Using pore strips
Only removes the top, root stays. Damages skin. Pores look bigger after.
🧽
Harsh scrubs / over-exfoliating
Micro-tears in skin. As defence, skin overproduces oil → MORE blackheads
🚿
Washing face more than 2x a day
Strips natural oils → skin panics and produces more oil → cycle worsens
☀️
Skipping sunscreen — even one day
1 day of UV exposure undoes weeks of skincare. SPF = non-negotiable.
💇 Hair Don'ts
🔥
Hot showers on scalp
Destroys scalp health, strips natural oils, causes dry and frizzy hair
🧣
Rubbing hair aggressively with towel
Causes breakage and frizz. Pat dry only, very gently.
🪮
Combing wet hair with fine-tooth comb
Hair is most fragile when wet. Use wide-tooth comb only.
🧴
Hair gels with alcohol / SLS shampoos daily
Dries and damages hair over time. Switch to mild SLS-free shampoo.
💦
Washing hair every day
Scalp needs its natural oils. Over-washing triggers more oil production.
🏃
Leaving post-run sweat on scalp for hours
Sweat + heat damages scalp. Rinse within 30–45 min after hard runs.
🏃 Running Don'ts
💨
Starting any race or run too fast
First 3 km determines last 3 km. Going out too fast = wall at the end
👟
Wearing new shoes on race day
Guaranteed blisters. Always race in broken-in shoes.
🧪
Trying new food/gel on race day
GI distress mid-race. Race with what you've trained with only.
🍽️
Eating a huge meal before a run
Cramps, nausea, sluggishness. 2 hrs min gap after any real meal.
