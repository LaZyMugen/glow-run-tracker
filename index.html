Yasso 800s
🔬Yasso 800s (Bart Yasso, Runner's World): Run 800m repeats with equal rest. The 800m time in mm:ss predicts your marathon finish time in hrs:mins. For 5K we use the same logic — your 800m rep time tells you where your race fitness sits.
📐Formula: Target 800m time = Goal 5K pace × 0.88. E.g. sub-27 5K = 5:24/km → target 800m = 4:45 per rep.
⚡Transition from short 400m bursts to sustained 800m efforts. This is where your lactate threshold gets seriously raised.

☀️ Before You Step In
0/0
▼
1
Brush / detangle hair before entering
Wet hair is 3× more fragile. Knots before = breakage during wash. Use wide-tooth comb.
2
Fill a mug with RO / filtered water for final hair rinse
The single most effective hard water fix. Keeps a bottle near your bucket/shower.
3
If oil massage day → apply oil 30 min before bath
Coconut or castor oil on scalp. Massage 15 min. Let sit 30 min then wash out. Do this Friday before Saturday wash.
4
If lemon / ACV rinse day → prep the mix now
2 tbsp lemon juice (or ACV) + 1 cup water in a mug. Set aside for step 9.
🚿 During Bath — Step by Step
0/0
▼
1
Wet entire body with lukewarm water LUKEWARM
Start here. Lukewarm opens pores for cleaning. Never start with hot.
2
Shampoo — scalp only, massage with fingertips HAIR WASH DAYS
Apply shampoo only to scalp. Massage gently with fingertips (not nails). Let sit 60 sec. The suds running down the lengths are enough for them.
3
Rinse shampoo fully — lukewarm LUKEWARM
Make sure zero shampoo residue remains. Residue = dandruff and itchy scalp.
4
Apply conditioner — mid-lengths to ends ONLY
Never apply conditioner to scalp — causes oiliness and clogged follicles. Just the lengths and ends. Leave in for 2–3 min.
5
Soap up body while conditioner sits
Use this 2–3 min productively. Lather soap on body, underarms, back. Let conditioner do its job simultaneously.
6
Rinse body soap thoroughly LUKEWARM
Soap residue on skin = pore clogging. Rinse underarms and back especially well.
7
Wash face with foam cleanser — cool water COOL
Use Cetaphil or Minimalist cleanser. Gentle circular motion 30 sec. Cool water — never hot on face. Rinse fully.
8
Rinse conditioner out — lukewarm LUKEWARM
Rinse until hair no longer feels slippery. Conditioner residue left in = greasy hair.
9
Pour lemon / ACV rinse over hair RINSE DAY ONLY
Pour the pre-mixed mug (2 tbsp lemon juice + 1 cup water) over scalp and hair. Leave 2 min. Neutralises hard water minerals.
10
Final cold blast on hair — 10 seconds COLD
Closes the hair cuticle. Locks in moisture. Seals shine. Makes hard water minerals less likely to deposit. Do this EVERY wash.
11
Pour filtered / RO water mug over hair as final rinse
The last thing to touch your hair should be clean water, not hard tap water. This one step dramatically reduces mineral buildup.
12
Cold splash on face — 30 seconds COLD
Right before stepping out. Reduces puffiness, tightens pores. Works synergistically with your skincare routine.
✨ After Bath — Don't Skip These
0/0
▼
1
Gently squeeze water out of hair — don't wring
Wringing twists and snaps wet hair. Squeeze downward with palms gently.
2
Pat hair dry with towel — never rub
Rubbing creates friction → frizz + breakage. Pat in sections. Hard water makes hair especially fragile when wet.
3
Pat face dry — immediately apply niacinamide serum
Apply while face is slightly damp — better absorption. Then moisturizer, then SPF (if morning).
4
Detangle hair with wide-tooth comb only
Start from ends, work upward to roots. Never drag from root down. If you have leave-in serum — apply now before combing.
5
Apply body moisturizer within 3 min of towel dry
The "3 minute rule" — skin retains moisture from the shower for about 3 min. Moisturizer applied in this window locks it in. Hard water especially dries skin out.
6
Let hair air dry — no heat
No hair dryer unless urgent. Hard water + heat = brittle, mineral-baked hair. Air dry naturally.
🧼 Soap & Body Wash Guide
✅ Use These
  • Dove — moisturising, mild, best daily bar for hard water. pH balanced.
  • Pears (transparent) — glycerin-rich, doesn't dry skin, gentle.
  • Cetaphil bar / wash — best option for sensitive/acne-prone skin.
  • Nivea Creme / body lotion bar — leaves skin less dry in hard water.
❌ Avoid These
  • Dettol daily — antibacterial but very drying. Use only when needed, not every day.
  • Lifebuoy — harsh surfactants, strips natural oils aggressively.
  • Highly fragranced soaps — irritate skin, especially in hard water areas.
  • Any soap with SLS as first ingredient — too stripping for daily use.
For body: soap the armpits, groin, feet, back thoroughly. The rest of the body just needs a water rinse — over-soaping everywhere dries skin unnecessarily.
🍋 ACV Alternatives — Hard Water Mineral Rinse
🍋
Fresh Lemon Juice Rinse FREE / ₹10

Best immediate alternative to ACV. Citric acid works the same way — neutralises alkaline mineral deposits, lowers hair pH, adds shine.

How: 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice + 1 cup water. Pour after conditioning, leave 2 min, rinse. 1× per week.

⚠️ Note: Don't use undiluted — too acidic. Always dilute. Avoid if you have scalp cuts/wounds.

🍚
Rice Water Rinse FREE

Ancient Indian remedy. Soak ½ cup raw rice in 2 cups water for 30 min. Strain. Use that water as a post-shampoo rinse.

Contains inositol — repairs hair damage, adds volume and shine. Also helps remove some mineral buildup.

How: Pour over hair after conditioner, leave 5 min, rinse. Use fermented version (soak 24 hrs, refrigerate) for stronger effect.

🍵
Green Tea Rinse ₹5 per use

Brew 2 green tea bags in 2 cups water, let cool fully. Use as final hair rinse after conditioner.

Mild acidity helps slightly with minerals. Main benefit: antioxidants for scalp health, reduces dandruff, adds mild shine.

How: Pour cooled tea over scalp and hair, leave 3 min, rinse with filtered water.

💧 Hard Water — Campus Survival Tips
🪣
Keep a 1-litre filtered / RO water bottle in your bathroom. Use it as the final pour over hair after every wash. Single biggest hair improvement for zero cost.
🧴
Use a chelating / clarifying shampoo once a week (Neutrogena Anti-Residue, Mamaearth Onion Shampoo). Removes mineral crust regular shampoo can't touch.
❄️
Always end hair wash with a cold rinse. Minerals deposit more on warm/open cuticles. Cold closes the cuticle and locks moisture in.
🪮
Hard water makes hair tangle more. Always brush before shower and use a wide-tooth comb only when wet. Never a fine-tooth comb on wet hair.
🧼
Hard water reduces soap lather. You'll use more soap than needed trying to lather. Use less soap, more water. Dove and glycerin bars lather better in hard water than cheap soaps.
💊
Hard water = dry skin always. Apply body moisturizer within 3 minutes of stepping out. Hard water strips the skin barrier more than soft water.
